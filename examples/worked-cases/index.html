One laptop can’t use the office printer

Kim’s laptop reports that its selected printer is offline. A second laptop prints to the office printer from the same room. Kim’s laptop lists both Office Printer and Office Printer (old). The old queue is selected.

Try it. Choose a first check and two ways to confirm the laptop is working again.

Compare the troubleshooting steps

Check the selected queue against the one that works on the second laptop. Select Office Printer, print a test page, then print Kim’s original document. If the queue still fails, record the exact error and compare the laptop’s connection before changing the shared printer.

Three rows for a weekly report

The report accepts each row with a date and sends undated rows for review. Each row ID identifies one entry, even if it is retried.

Sample input
RowDateUnits
AMay 43
BMissing2
CMay 64

Try it. Find the initial total and decide what happens when B gets a date.

Compare the report results

A and C give seven units. B goes to review. After checking B’s date, retry that row. The report then shows nine units across the three IDs. Retrying A or C leaves the total at nine.

Turn sample meeting notes into next steps

Notes. Lee offered to check the sample page. The team also discussed a short welcome email. Nobody took that task or gave a deadline.

Sample AI output. “Lee checks the sample page on Friday. Morgan writes the welcome email by Tuesday.”

Try it. Mark which owners and deadlines the notes actually support.

Compare the corrected action list

Lee checks the sample page. The deadline is open. The welcome email still needs an owner and a date. Ask the team to confirm both before assigning the work.
