Questions before you begin

The Foundation is intentionally bounded. These answers make the fit, deliverables, price, responsibilities, and clean no-fit stop explicit.

Who is the Foundation for?

One small business or operating department of roughly 5–20 people with a sponsor who can provide access and support one bounded experiment.

What happens during the engagement?

Sponsor intake, one six-hour onsite discovery day, synthesis within five business days, and one remote leadership readout, within an approximately sixteen-hour practitioner boundary.

What does the founding engagement cost?

$2,500 for each of the first two external engagements, subject to a one-page proposal.

What do we receive?

A team-and-role map, AI-use baseline, potential AI Champion map, ranked opportunity backlog, starter responsible-use guardrails, one recommended 90-day experiment, leadership brief, and editable handoff materials.

Does the Foundation include implementation?

No. Production implementation, custom software, infrastructure, training, maintenance, managed support, and specialist assurance are outside the Foundation. Any later work requires a separate evaluation and proposal.

Can we use the Field Guide ourselves?

Yes. The guides provide complete bounded procedures and downloadable templates. Bring in facilitation when ownership, cross-functional disagreement, sensitive data, specialist review, or irreversible decisions make a neutral process valuable.

Is this a security, legal, privacy, or compliance assessment?

No. The Foundation includes starter responsible-use guardrails and flags questions for qualified specialists; it is not an audit or assurance service.

What happens if the Foundation is not a fit?

The process stops cleanly. It is not a fit for a production application, organization-wide transformation, staff-reduction program, specialist assessment, or guaranteed return.
