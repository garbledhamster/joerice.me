The situation

When the Foundation may fit

The Foundation may fit a team with uneven AI use, scattered experimentation, uncertainty about responsible use, or recurring work friction.

  • AI use is uneven across the team.
  • AI experimentation is scattered across the team.
  • The team is uncertain about responsible use.
  • Repeated work friction creates delay, rework, risk, frustration, or avoidable cost.

The engagement

Listen, map, rank, choose, and hand off

I learn how the team is organized, establish a current AI-use baseline, identify potential AI Champions, and surface repeated pain points.

  1. Listen

    Learn how the team is organized and where repeated pain points create delay, rework, risk, frustration, or avoidable cost.

  2. Map

    Establish the current AI-use baseline and identify potential AI Champions—department-aware employees interested in helping discover, evaluate, and eventually support useful practices, not a certification or formal title.

  3. Rank

    Together, rank opportunities by value, feasibility, risk, and readiness. A pain point is not automatically an AI or automation opportunity.

  4. Choose

    Select one bounded 90-day experiment as the recommended next step.

  5. Hand off

    Receive editable materials designed for your team to continue without ongoing dependence on me.

The handoff

What you receive

Eight concrete outputs, including editable handoff materials.

Team-and-role map

A view of the team structure and roles considered during discovery.

AI-use baseline

A current view of how AI use varies across the team.

Potential AI Champion map

Department-aware people who may help discover, evaluate, and eventually support useful practices.

Ranked opportunity backlog

An editable list of observed pain points and possible AI or automation responses, ranked by value, feasibility, risk, and readiness.

Starter responsible-use guardrails

Starter guidance based on the observed context, including questions that need legal, privacy, security, or compliance specialists.

One recommended 90-day experiment

A bounded experiment selected after the opportunities are ranked.

Leadership brief

A concise readout for the sponsor and leadership team.

Editable handoff materials

Working materials designed for the team to take forward.

Fit and no-fit

A bounded engagement, by design

May fit

  • One small business or operating department of roughly 5–20 people.
  • A team facing uneven AI use, scattered experimentation, uncertainty about responsible use, or recurring work friction.
  • A sponsor ready to support one bounded 90-day experiment after the handoff.

Not a fit

This is not a fit for a production application, security assessment, compliance assurance, organization-wide transformation, staff-reduction program, or guaranteed return.

The practitioner

A clear learning and responsibility boundary

I am a solo, security-conscious technology practitioner actively learning, refining this offer, and validating it through founding engagements.

The engagement includes starter responsible-use guardrails and flags questions that require legal, privacy, security, or compliance specialists. It is not an audit or assurance service.

The founding offer

Price, delivery, and exclusions

Founding engagement

$2,500

Founding price for each of the first two external engagements, subject to a one-page proposal.

Delivery boundary

Sponsor intake, one six-hour onsite discovery day, synthesis within five business days, and one remote leadership readout, within an approximately sixteen-hour practitioner boundary.

The proposal controls the final schedule, access, payment, confidentiality, and cancellation terms.

What the Foundation does not include

  • Production implementation, custom automation, or software development
  • Hosted infrastructure, cloud migration, or an internal LLM environment
  • Ongoing maintenance or managed support
  • Formal certification or training
  • Security assessment, legal advice, or compliance assurance
  • Guaranteed savings, adoption, return, or other business outcomes

Automation implementation, custom systems, infrastructure, training, maintenance, and support are not included. If later work is justified, it is evaluated and proposed separately.
