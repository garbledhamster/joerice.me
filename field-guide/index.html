The Field Guide

Practical guides for making one sound AI decision at a time

Use the complete procedures yourself. Bring in facilitation when ownership, risk, sensitive data, or cross-functional disagreement makes neutral judgment valuable.

  1. Observe the work
  2. Map evidence
  3. Rank options
  4. Run one experiment
  5. Decide what follows

listen

Run a lightweight AI enablement discovery

Learn what is actually happening in one team's work before choosing tools, policies, or projects.

rank

Rank AI opportunities without chasing every pain point

Turn observed work friction into a transparent shortlist using value, feasibility, risk, and readiness.

choose

Design a bounded 90-day AI experiment

Define a reversible learning cycle with an owner, baseline, guardrails, checkpoints, and an explicit stop decision.

How this stays current

Ideas enter the canonical draft first. Factual claims keep their sources, unresolved questions remain visible, and only reviewed guides marked ready enter the public build. Published guidance receives an event-driven update when evidence changes and a quarterly review.

Joe authors and maintains this Field Guide with AI-assisted research and editing. Factual guidance links to its sources; working procedures are presented as practical methods, not guaranteed outcomes.
